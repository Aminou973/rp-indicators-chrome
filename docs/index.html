Your rP ranges, on every chart.

The rP range 3/5/60 indicator and the screener, pulled out of TradingView and onto Vest, NinjaTrader Web, TopstepX and other web terminals. Same rules, same look, live data from the chart you're already on.

Download the extension View on GitHub

NQ on Vest, 1m chart. 3m / 5m / 60m ranges drawn like the Pine, panel top right.

What you get

The ranges on the chart

3m, 5m and 60m step lines with H, L and 50%, the 60m 40–60% zone, ▲/▼ flips, L / S setups and the labels on the right.

The screener

NQ, ES, YM, RTY, GC, CL, BTC: 3m / 5m / 60m direction, which 60m levels got touched, setup and sweep status.

No-trade warnings

04:00 move, equity open, and CPI / NFP / PPI / FOMC days, plus a countdown to the next one.

Display only

It reads and draws. It never places, moves or cancels an order.

The rules, quickly

Range

A candle that closes beyond the range starts a new range in that direction, built from that candle's high and low. Wicks don't count.

Setup

Price touches the 60m 40, 50 or 60%. Then, within 2 hours, the 3m or 5m flips in the 60m direction.

Sweep

Price trades past a 60m edge against its direction, then a 3m or 5m flip closes back inside.

Install

  1. Download the zip and unzip it somewhere you'll keep it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  4. Open your chart. The panel shows up top right, drag it wherever you like. On a site it doesn't know, click the extension icon.

Updating: replace the folder, hit reload on the extension, refresh your chart tabs. The version shows in the panel header.

Make it yours

Colors, the 40/60 levels, the setup window, sessions, news dates, your symbol list: it's all plain JavaScript, no build step. CUSTOMIZE.md says where each setting lives, and it has a prompt you can hand to Claude or any AI to make the change for you.

Good to know

  • The screener rows for futures use a free public feed that runs about 10 minutes behind. The symbol on your chart is live where the platform allows it.
  • On a 3m chart with TradingView or Vest, the 5m range can't be built from 3m candles. Use a 1m chart and everything is live.
  • The news calendar runs to December 2027.
